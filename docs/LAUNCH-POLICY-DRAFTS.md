### Extraction, AI and sharing

We store originals and results to provide extraction, comparison, correction, approval and export. Bank-statement extraction uses AI, including for native/text statements. When AI extraction, field suggestions or split suggestions are used, relevant document text and/or file/image content, field definitions and instructions are sent to OpenAI. TIFF processing can send rendered page images in a PDF. Generic rules/template workflows can avoid an AI call when they successfully match; that is not a promise that every workflow is AI-free.

The application uses `store:false` for its OpenAI Responses requests and does not implement a separate cross-workspace training process using customer corrections. This is **not** a zero-retention, EU-only-processing or provider-training assurance. OpenAI documents separate abuse-monitoring retention and account-specific controls; the actual account settings and agreement still need verification. [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

Authorised workspace members can access data according to their roles. Our hosting, storage and processing providers receive the data needed for their functions. If you enable incoming email, Sheets or webhooks, the relevant provider/destination also receives the configured content. Downloads and destination copies remain under their respective controls. The provider table in section 3 must identify the actual arrangements before publication.

Extraction can be wrong. The product provides review and explicit approval; it does not itself decide credit, eligibility or another legal/significant outcome about a person. **[Confirm no separate operator/customer deployment introduces such decision-making.]** The service is document-processing software, not financial, accounting or legal advice.
